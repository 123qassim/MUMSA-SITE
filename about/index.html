About MUMSA

Faith, unity and service at Maseno University.

MUMSA is the student community that brings Muslim students at Maseno University together to worship, learn, support one another and contribute positively to university life.

Who we are

A home for Muslim students

We are a campus-based association serving Muslim students across Maseno University. MUMSA creates welcoming spaces where students can strengthen their Islamic identity, build friendships, seek support and take part in purposeful activities.

Our vision

Students who grow with faith and purpose.

To see a united, confident and excellent Muslim student community that lives its faith, develops good character and contributes meaningfully to the university and society.

Our mission

To strengthen faith and serve students.

We support Muslim students through Islamic learning, academic encouragement, welfare initiatives, leadership development and service grounded in Islamic values.

Our objectives

Turning our purpose into practical support.

Facilitate Islamic learning

Provide regular opportunities for halaqahs, talks, reflection and beneficial knowledge.

Encourage academic excellence

Promote study, peer encouragement and habits that help Muslim students flourish.

Provide welfare support

Listen to student needs and connect members with care, guidance and practical support.

Develop responsible leaders

Give students opportunities to lead, volunteer, collaborate and serve with integrity.

Our values

How we show up for one another.

  • FaithWe keep Islamic guidance at the heart of our work.
  • UnityWe build belonging across backgrounds, courses and years.
  • ExcellenceWe pursue growth in worship, study, character and service.
  • CompassionWe care for students with respect, dignity and empathy.
  • ResponsibilityWe lead honestly and contribute to the wider university community.
Our history

Built by generations of Muslim students.

MUMSA grows from the longstanding effort of Muslim students at Maseno University to pray together, learn together and support one another. Each generation of students adds to that foundation through programmes, welfare initiatives, leadership and service.

Our story is still being written by the students who participate today: learning from those who came before, responding to present needs and preparing a stronger community for those who follow.

Our relationship with Maseno University

Rooted in the university community.

MUMSA is a student association within Maseno University. We work as part of the university community, encouraging Muslim students to participate positively in academic, cultural, welfare and service life while observing university policies and respecting the diversity of the campus.

Our commitment to Muslim students

Every Muslim student deserves a community that helps them thrive.

We are committed to welcoming students, supporting their wellbeing, nurturing good character and making it easier to find faith, friendship, learning and leadership at Maseno University.

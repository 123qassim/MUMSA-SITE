MUMSA introduction

You do not have to navigate campus alone.

MUMSA brings Muslim students together for worship, friendship, learning, welfare and leadership. Whether you need directions, a familiar face, academic encouragement or a place to pray, you can begin by connecting with the MUMSA community.

Frequently asked questions

Quick answers for your first weeks.

How do I join MUMSA?

Start with the MUMSA registration page, then attend a programme or gathering to meet the community.

Join MUMSA →
Where can I find prayer information?

Check the Maseno Masjid noticeboard and official MUMSA announcements for the current prayer timetable and mosque notices.

What if I do not know anyone at Maseno?

Come to a MUMSA event, introduce yourself to a committee member or contact us. The First-Year Hub exists to help you find a starting point.

Where can I get academic or welfare help?

Contact MUMSA for community guidance and use the relevant official Maseno University office for academic, accommodation or professional welfare support.

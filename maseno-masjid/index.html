Masjid guidelines

Respect the house of Allah.

Keep phones quiet and maintain a peaceful environment for worship.

Dress modestly and observe the etiquette of the masjid.

Keep the prayer spaces, ablution areas and surrounding grounds clean.

Respect worshippers, volunteers, university policies and masjid instructions.
